# Code of Conduct

tobrod is a small learning project. Everyone who takes part – in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else in this repository – should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their experience, background or identity.

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here are learning – including the maintainer.
- Assume good intent, and explain instead of judging. A question that seems obvious is still a fair question.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Accept feedback on your own work in the same way.
- Keep discussions on topic, and respect a "no" or a "not now".

## What is not okay

- Insults, demeaning or discriminatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a friendly, professional setting

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that isn't okay, please tell the maintainer, Sören Lemke ([@soerenlemke](https://github.com/soerenlemke)), privately – the contact options are on the GitHub profile. Reports are treated confidentially.

## What happens then

The maintainer looks into every report and decides what to do: a note, a request to change something, editing or removing comments, or – for serious or repeated problems – a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

This applies to everything in this repository and to anyone who represents the project elsewhere.
